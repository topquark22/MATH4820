# Lecture Notes

This directory holds the lecture notes and plans for MATH 4820 / CS 6820. Files are named `weekNN-dayM.md`.

## Scheduling assumptions

- **Lectures:** Two 90-minute lectures per week (Day 1 and Day 2), i.e. 3 contact hours of lecture.
- **Tutorials:** Two 60-minute tutorial sessions per week, taught by a teaching assistant (TA). Tutorials support the lectures (worked problems, lab help).

Both are assumptions. Actual lecture/tutorial length and frequency vary by institution and scheduling; adjust the plans accordingly. Note that `syllabus/course-overview.md` lists "3 lecture hours and 1 lab hour per week" (confirm locally), which does not exactly match this assumption.

## Contents

- [Week 1, Day 1](week01-day1.md): Images as functions and arrays; injective/surjective/bijective operators
- [Week 1, Day 2](week01-day2.md): Matrix refresher; coordinate grids; resampling and interpolation
- [Week 2, Day 1](week02-day1.md): Affine maps, homographies, pixel permutations, resampling consequences
- [Week 2, Day 2](week02-day2.md): Swirl/twirl warps, inversion, parameter estimation, brief intro to *Operation Vico*
- [Week 3, Day 1](week03-day1.md): Color spaces, chroma subsampling, 8×8 block DCT
- [Week 3, Day 2](week03-day2.md): Quantization and information loss; double-JPEG and resampling traces
- [Week 4, Day 1](week04-day1.md): DFT/FFT fundamentals (1D and 2D DFT, FFT algorithm, reading spectra)
- [Week 4, Day 2](week04-day2.md): PSFs, spatial convolution, the Convolution Theorem, circular vs. linear convolution, zeros and attenuation
