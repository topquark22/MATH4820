# References and Resources

## Web-Scale Visual Privacy and Generative Anonymization

- Frome, A., Cheung, G., Abdulkader, A., Zennaro, M., Wu, B., Bissacco, A., Adam, H., Neven, H., & Vincent, L. (2009). *Large-Scale Privacy Protection in Google Street View.* In Proceedings of the 2009 IEEE 12th International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), pp. 2373–2380.
  - Paper (source Gist link): [Google Research Archive](https://google.com)
  - Publication record: [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5459413/)
- Hukkelås, H., Mester, R., & Lindseth, F. (2019). *DeepPrivacy: A Generative Adversarial Network for Face Anonymization.* In Advances in Visual Computing (ISVC 2019), Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol. 11844, pp. 565–578. Springer.
  - Preprint: [arXiv:1909.04538](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.04538)
  - Source code and weights (license as listed in source Gist): [hukkelas/DeepPrivacy](https://github.com/hukkelas/DeepPrivacy)
- Hukkelås, H., & Lindseth, F. (2023). *DeepPrivacy2: Towards Realistic Full-Body Anonymization.* In Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV), pp. 1329–1338.
  - Paper: [CVF Open Access](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2023/papers/Hukkelas_DeepPrivacy2_Towards_Realistic_Full-Body_Anonymization_WACV_2023_paper.pdf) | [arXiv:2211.09454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09454)
  - Source code and weights (license as listed in source Gist): [hukkelas/deep_privacy2](https://github.com/hukkelas/deep_privacy2)

## Foundational Signal Processing and Inverse Problems

- Gonzalez, R. C., & Woods, R. E. *Digital Image Processing* (4th ed.). Pearson. Chapters on geometric transformations, image restoration, and regularized deconvolution.
- Bertero, M., Boccacci, P., & De Mol, C. *Introduction to Inverse Problems in Imaging* (2nd ed.). CRC Press. Mathematical treatment of ill-posedness, Hadamard conditions, and Tikhonov / Wiener / Richardson–Lucy regularization.
