## Materials and integrity

## Examination question and assumptions

State the feature or inference under examination, observation model, relevant limitations and alternative explanations. Distinguish investigative objectives from evidentiary claims.

## Method and reproducibility

Describe transformations, software and model/checkpoint versions, settings, numerical conventions, execution environment and output identifiers. Distinguish original materials, processed derivatives and synthesized content.

## Validation

Document independent controls, calibration and evaluation separation, baseline methods, decision rules, confusion counts, failures, uncertainty and model mismatch. Explain applicability to the exercise conditions.

## Findings

Separate directly observed properties, numerical findings, model-dependent inferences and unresolved features. Cite supporting figures and records. Identify any demonstrated errors without inferring unobserved original details.

## Limitations and conclusions

State the scope of the conclusions and what the available evidence cannot establish. Do not claim universal reliability or admissibility.
