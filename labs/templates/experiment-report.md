## Question and mathematical model

State the quantity to be estimated or protected, the forward or release operator, the assumptions, and the intended operating domain.

## Data and experimental design

Describe synthetic or authorized inputs, data scale, coordinate and boundary conventions, reference provenance, independent sampling unit, calibration/test separation, and what was withheld. Record the configuration before test scoring.

## Implementation and verification

Describe the algorithm, parameters, relevant software versions, numerical tolerances, and public sanity checks. Include instructions to reproduce the run.

## Results

Provide a labelled table of predeclared conditions, baselines, metrics, units, denominators, uncertainty and failures. Distinguish unclipped numerical estimates from display derivatives. Include figures with attribution and captions as appropriate.

## Interpretation

Explain whether the results support the mathematical hypothesis, distinguish implementation error from model limitations, and identify any alternate explanations. Separate measured findings from assumptions and unsupported claims.

## Reproducibility and limitations

Record output identifiers or digests, relevant random seeds, deviations, unavailable measurements, and proposed follow-up experiments. Do not include credentials, private identifiers or confidential assessment data in public submissions.
